In KukuGuard our KNN-OOD cosine gate used a static threshold of -0.4585 tuned for 95% validation TPR against training prototypes from clean substrate benchmarks. Note clean_soils already had the weakest rejection (92% vs 100% on hands and boots, mean cosine -0.594). Under midday glare or lens smear, low-frequency contrast washes out the backbone embeddings, collapsing intra-class norms and pulling clean clinic images closer to in-distribution cluster centroids—simultaneously letting blurry OOD inputs pass the -0.4585 cutoff and forcing borderline valid scans into the paid review queue. Raising the cutoff alone breaks recall below 95% TPR, violating sensitivity constraints. Instead I would keep KNN-OOD as Stage 2 only after a cheap deterministic Laplacian-variance and exposure histogram gate on budget Android (~2ms OpenCV) rejects blurry frames for free recapture, and re-fit separate per-clinic prototype clusters or a cosine + SVM-margin two-key threshold calibrated on clinic illumination shifts to stay inside the $0.02/scan budget.
