To protect review margins at scale in edge diagnostics, the key architectural priority is orchestrating a principled confidence calibration pipeline that decouples uncertainty quantification from downstream human escalation SLAs. First, we should institute continuous drift observability across incoming image telemetry so threshold policies adapt gracefully as real-world distributions evolve. Second, combining calibrated confidence intervals with hierarchical routing ensures high-certainty inferences resolve autonomously at zero marginal cost while genuinely ambiguous boundary cases trigger selective human-in-the-loop review. By establishing feedback loops from reviewer annotations back into active learning queues, organizations systematically lower false positive escalation rates, preserve clinician throughput, and align model precision directly with unit economics over time.
